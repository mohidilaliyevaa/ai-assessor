## Essay 1: o'rtacha

**Task:** Some people think students should wear school uniforms. Do you agree? (180-200 words)

**Essay:**
I think school uniforms are good idea. First, when all students wear the same clothes, nobody feel poor or rich. This is very important for childrens, because some families can't buy expensive clothes and their kids can be sad. Second, uniforms saves time in the morning because students dont need to choose what to wear. Parents also spend less money, as they buy only one or two sets of clothes for the whole year. Third, uniforms help students to concentrate on lessons instead of fashion. Last year my school introduced uniforms and I was very happy about it. Everyone looked tidy and the classroom became more disciplined. However, some people say that uniforms are boring and students can't express theirselves. I disagree because they can express them by hobbies, music and sport. Students can also show their personality with their ideas and their behaviour, not only with their clothes. Another argument against uniforms is that they are uncomfortable in hot weather, but schools can choose light and soft fabrics. In conclusion, I am agree with uniforms in schools, because they bring equality, save money and create a serious atmosphere for study.

**Kalit (8 ta xato):**
- `are good idea` → a good idea
- `nobody feel` → nobody feels
- `childrens` → children
- `uniforms saves` → uniforms save
- `dont` → don't
- `theirselves` → themselves
- `express them by hobbies` → express themselves through hobbies
- `I am agree` → I agree

## Essay 2: kuchli (xatosiz)

**Task:** Do you think online learning is better than classroom learning? (180-200 words)

**Essay:**
Online learning has become increasingly popular in recent years, and I believe it offers more advantages than disadvantages. Firstly, students can study at their own pace and review difficult lessons as many times as they need. In a traditional classroom, a teacher must move on with the programme, so some learners are left behind. Secondly, online learning saves money, since there is no need to pay for transport or accommodation. This is especially valuable for students who live far from large cities. Moreover, they can choose from thousands of courses taught by experts from different countries, which would be impossible in a small local school. However, online learning is not perfect. Some students find it hard to stay motivated without a teacher in the room, and they may be distracted by social media or games. In addition, practical skills such as laboratory work or speaking practice are difficult to develop through a screen. Overall, I think the best solution is a combination of both methods. This approach allows students to benefit from flexibility and low cost while still receiving personal support and face-to-face communication, which remain essential for healthy learning and social development.

## Essay 3: o'rtacha

**Task:** Is it better to live in a city or in a village? (180-200 words)

**Essay:**
Many people prefer living in the city because there are more jobs and better hospitals. Young people also have a chance to study at good universities and meet interesting people. In my opinion, city life have some problem too. For example, the air is dirty and traffic is very bad. People spend two or three hours in buses every day, and they are always tired and nervous. Housing is another difficulty, because flats in big cities are very expensive. Last summer I visit my grandmother's village and I felt very relax. The weather was warm, the mountains were beautiful and nobody was in a hurry. People there are more friendly and the food is more fresh, because it comes directly from the garden. My grandmother grows tomatoes, cucumbers and apples, and we ate them every day. But in the village there is less opportunities for young people. There are few good schools, and it is difficult to find a modern job. Many teenagers leave their villages after they finish school. I think the best choice depend on person's age and job. A student may need the city, but a retired person may prefer a quiet life in the countryside.

**Kalit (8 ta xato):**
- `life have` → life has
- `some problem` → some problems
- `I visit` → I visited
- `very relax` → very relaxed
- `more fresh` → fresher
- `less opportunities` → fewer opportunities
- `depend` → depends
- `on person's` → on a person's (yoki: depends on the person's)

## Essay 4: o'rtacha

**Task:** What are the advantages and disadvantages of social media for teenagers? (180-200 words)

**Essay:**
Nowadays teenagers spend a lot of time in social media. It has both advantages and disadvantages. On the one hand, they can communicate with friends who lives far away and learn new things. Many young people watch educational videos, read news and even find useful information for their studies. Social media can also help them to discover new hobbies and make friends with similar interests. On the other hand, they sometimes waste their time and doesn't do their homework. They stay awake late at night and feel sleepy at school the next day. In addition, bullying in the internet is a serious problem. Some teenagers receive rude messages from strangers, and this can make them feel lonely and afraid. Another danger is that they believe everything they see online, although a lot of information is not true. Parents should control how much time their children spends online. They should also talk to them about safe behaviour and explain which websites are dangerous. In my opinion, social media is not bad if teenagers use it carefully. The most important thing is to find a balance between online life and real communication with family and friends.

**Kalit (5 ta xato):**
- `in social media` → on social media
- `friends who lives` → friends who live
- `and doesn't do` → and don't do
- `in the internet` → on the internet
- `children spends` → children spend

## Essay 5: zaif

**Task:** Write about what you did last weekend. (180-200 words)

**Essay:**
Last weekend I go to my friend house. We play football and then we eat lunch in cafe. The food was very delicious but expensive. After that we goes to cinema and watch a film about animals. It was very interesting film. I come back home at 9 o'clock and I was tired. My mother ask me where I was and I tell her everything. On Sunday I wake up late, because I was very tired. I have breakfast with my family and then I help my father in the garden. We plant three small tree near the house. My little sister want to help us too, but she was too small. In the afternoon I do my homework and read a book about history. It was boring book, but I must to finish it before Monday. In the evening my grandfather call me and we talk about my school. He ask me many questions and I answer him. After dinner I watch TV with my brother and then I go to bed early. It was a good weekend and I am very happy. I want go to my friend house again next weekend.
